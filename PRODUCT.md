# LifeWorld Product Specification v0.1

## Product Goal

LifeWorld is a personal world that grows with your life.

Users do not manage tasks.
Users live their life.

The world reflects their meaningful actions.

---

# Core Experience

Every night, users return to their world.

They see how their life has shaped it.

The feeling we want:

"I am becoming the person I want to be."

---

# MVP Version

The first version only has one core loop.

## User Flow

1. User opens the app.

2. User sees their world.

3. User tells AI what happened today.

Example:

"Today I worked until 11pm, but still practiced French for 20 minutes."

4. AI understands the meaning.

5. The world changes slightly.

6. User leaves with a feeling of progress.

---

# Main Screen

The first screen is the world.

Not the chat.

Not the task list.

Not the AI.

The world is the product.

---

# World Rules

The user does not manually build the world.

The world grows automatically.

Examples:

Learning:
- Library grows.
- Books appear.

Exercise:
- Training area develops.
- Sports facilities improve.

Travel:
- Roads and airports expand.

Family:
- Garden becomes warmer.

Work:
- Workshop develops.

---

# AI Role

AI is an observer.

AI should:

- Understand user input.
- Identify meaningful moments.
- Explain changes.

AI should never:

- Judge users.
- Punish users.
- Create pressure.

---

# Daily Interaction

Question:

"How was your day?"

Not:

"What tasks did you complete?"

---

# First Version World

The MVP only contains:

- A small home.
- A piece of land.
- A few areas that can grow.

Do not build:

- Complex cities.
- NPC systems.
- Multiplayer.
- Ranking systems.

---

# Success Metric

The first goal is not downloads.

The first goal:

A user opens the app after 30 days and thinks:

"I want to see what my world has become."

---

# Product Principle

Users live.

The world remembers.
